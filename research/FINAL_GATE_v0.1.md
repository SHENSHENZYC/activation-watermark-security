## 3. The lead read in full: Chen et al., arXiv:2610.04907v1 (15 pp; "Under review as a conference paper at ICLR 2027" printed; CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **What it does (verbatim, PDF pages):** "Our testbed is subliminal steering: the teacher trait is a known residual-stream vector ∆T, so transfer can be measured directly as parameter recovery" (p. 1); "Locally, the carrier task maps the trait through its Fisher matrix F, so gradients point toward F∆T rather than ∆T. Gradient descent then acts as a progressively less-damped inverse of F" (p. 1); "Distributional supervision recovers ∆T almost perfectly from only hundreds of examples" (p. 2); at zero optimisation steps "The recovered direction is therefore F∆T" (p. 8). Models Qwen2.5-7B and Gemma-2-9B (p. 1).
- **Absences (searched in the extraction):** the words watermark, attack, adversar-, secret and hidden state do not occur.
- **Relation to our claims.** The paper states, in a non-adversarial distillation setting, the first-order relation that §3 of our paper uses as its inference (E[g] ≈ F v) and that Route A′ exploits by averaging the standardised first gradient over observed outputs; their student then inverts F by iterating, which our attacker does not. It measures no watermark, detector, forgery, scrubbing, key test or keyed defence, so it occupies no part of C1–C13; it was posted on 2026-10-04, after every study here was locked and run. **Action taken:** one labelled sentence in §6's score-test paragraph ("Concurrently and outside watermarking, …"), a `references.bib` entry (`chen2026subliminal`; author order from the cover), this record. No claim or wording rule changes.
