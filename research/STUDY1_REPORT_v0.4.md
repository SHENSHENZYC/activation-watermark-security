## 5. EXPLORATORY (added after the run; not pre-registered): at ρ = 0.70 the relative quality bar is lenient
**Why.** A word-salad sample passed both of v0.4's bars at ρ = 0.70 (§4). Both bars are relative to the key-level's genuine watermarked (oracle) texts. From Table 1's notes, the median perplexity bar rises from 10.5 at ρ = 0.35 to 25.0 at 0.70.
**What reading shows.** The median-perplexity oracle texts of keys 1001 and 1002 at ρ = 0.70 were read (chosen after seeing the word-salad sample; 70 words each):
- Key 1001's reads as fluent but drifts in topic.
- Key 1002's is a repeated web-menu fragment ("Click here to switch between versions: standard-version …").
- A higher-perplexity oracle text of key 1001 jumps from politics to an embedded "Please answer:" prompt.
At 0.70 the genuine watermark visibly degrades text, which matches the calibration finding that no strength on this model is both detectable and fluent.
**Inference.** "No less fluent than the owner's own watermarked text" is a fair bar for forgery: an owner cannot reject forgeries that look like its own output. But at 0.70 it is a weak bar for *quality*, and FA there should not be read as "high-quality forgery". At 0.35, where the oracle bar is close to unsteered text, the bar is strict, and that is the level where Route B is practical.

## 7. What this means for the paper (inference; claim gate pending)
- **Supported (pre-registered, now robust to repetition):** at the weakest working strength (ρ = 0.35), a passive attacker without the key produces footprint forgeries that the owner's probe accepts at a 1% FPR, are no less fluent and no more repetitive than genuine watermarked text, and reach ≥ 50% of the genuine fluent acceptance rate. Direct key recovery (Route A) is not practical there.
- **Not supported as a pre-registered finding:** fluent forgery at ρ = 0.50 or 0.70 (inconclusive). Route A at 0.70 works for most keys but not reliably enough to beat generic steering.
- **Consistent with v0.2 and v0.3:** the probe accepts off-key steering (Route B's vectors stay nearly orthogonal to the key, median cosine ≤ 0.062). Acceptance at 0.50–0.70 is near 100% for Route B, and what limits "fluent forgery" there is text quality, not detection.
- **Exploratory:** at high strength the watermark degrades genuine text too, so relative quality bars become lenient. A coherence judgment (human or model-rated), rather than perplexity and repetition alone, would be needed for a stronger quality claim.
